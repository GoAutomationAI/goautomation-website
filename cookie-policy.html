Cookie Policy

Version 1.6  ·  Effective 19 August 2026  ·  Company No. 14400611

01What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to website owners. Cookies do not give us access to your device and we cannot use them to identify you personally unless you have provided us with personal information.

02How We Use Cookies

Our website (goautomation.ai) uses cookies to improve your browsing experience, understand how visitors use our site, and to support our marketing activity. Non-essential cookies — including the Meta and LinkedIn advertising cookies described below — are not placed on your device unless and until you accept them.

03Types of Cookies We Use

Strictly Necessary

Essential for the website to function. Cannot be disabled.

No consent required

Performance / Analytics

Help us understand how visitors use our site, such as pages visited and time spent. Data is aggregated and anonymous.

Consent required

Functional

Remember your preferences and settings to improve your experience.

Consent required

Marketing

Used to measure the performance of our advertising and to track conversions, such as a completed booking. These cookies are set by Meta and LinkedIn and may be used to build advertising audiences.

Consent required

04Third-Party Cookies

  • Meta Pixel — advertising measurement and conversion tracking, present across our website
  • LinkedIn Insight Tag — advertising measurement and conversion tracking, present on our booking confirmation page
  • GoHighLevel — our embedded booking calendar and forms

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies. We recommend reviewing them directly if you have concerns.

05Managing and Withdrawing Consent

When you first visit our website you will be shown a banner asking whether you accept non-essential cookies. Nothing non-essential is set until you choose “Accept”, and if you choose “Decline” no advertising cookies are placed at all.

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of any page on this site, which reopens the banner. You can also clear or block cookies through your browser, as described in the next section.

06How to Control Cookies in Your Browser

  • Google Chrome — support.google.com/chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox — support.mozilla.org
  • Safari — support.apple.com
  • Microsoft Edge — support.microsoft.com

07Retention of Cookie Data

Session cookies are deleted when you close your browser. Persistent cookies remain for a set period — typically between 30 days and 2 years — unless deleted earlier.

08Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation or our services. The current version and effective date are shown at the top of this document.
