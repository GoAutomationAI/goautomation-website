Data Processing Agreement

UK GDPR Article 28  ·  Version 1.15  ·  Effective 24 October 2026

01Purpose

This Data Processing Agreement ("Agreement") forms part of the agreement between Go Automation AI Ltd ("Processor") and the Client ("Controller"). It sets out the obligations of both parties in accordance with Article 28 of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) where Go Automation AI Ltd processes personal data on behalf of the Controller.

02Subject Matter

Go Automation AI Ltd provides AI-powered business automation services including CRM automation, customer communications, document processing, workflow automation, appointment booking and related software services. Processing of personal data is limited to the delivery of those contracted services.

03Duration

This Agreement remains in force for as long as Go Automation AI Ltd processes personal data on behalf of the Controller under the Service Agreement.

04Nature and Purpose of Processing

  • CRM automation and AI-powered customer communications
  • WhatsApp Business messaging and SMS messaging, sent via the Controller's own connected WhatsApp Business Account, including electronic marketing communications instructed by the Controller
  • Document classification, summarisation, data extraction and fact verification
  • Protection and insurance fact-find, including AI-assisted processing of medical and lifestyle information provided by the Controller's customers
  • Lead management and workflow automation
  • Appointment scheduling and calendar synchronisation
  • Where the Controller sends Go Automation a spreadsheet of its customers' contact details for import into the Platform's CRM, checking that data for accuracy (for example, values that have shifted between columns) before import
  • Customer support, reporting and administration
  • Generation and indefinite retention of PII-free, document-type-level descriptions (and their numerical embeddings) derived from confirmed documents, used to improve classification accuracy for the Controller over time. These descriptions are designed to exclude all personal data values and do not identify any data subject.

05Categories of Personal Data

  • Full name, email address, telephone number, postal address
  • Property, case, or engagement details relevant to the Controller's services
  • Financial information contained in documents submitted for processing (for example, bank statements)
  • Identification documents submitted for processing
  • CRM records, lead information, customer notes
  • Special category data: health and medical information, and lifestyle information (for example, smoking status, family medical history), where provided by a data subject as part of a protection or insurance fact-find journey. Source documents and communications may also incidentally contain special category information not intentionally solicited (for example, a bank statement showing a payment to a religious, political, or medical organisation, or a data subject volunteering health information during an unrelated conversation).
  • Other information supplied by the Controller

06Categories of Data Subjects

  • The Controller's existing and prospective customers
  • The Controller's employees, business contacts and introducers
  • Other individuals whose information is supplied by the Controller

07Controller Obligations

The Client (Controller) retains full responsibility for compliance with UK GDPR throughout. Go Automation AI Ltd acts solely as a Data Processor, processing personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller. The Controller confirms that it has an appropriate lawful basis for processing, has provided all necessary privacy information to data subjects, and remains accountable as Data Controller at all times — including for any onward transfer instructions given to Go Automation AI Ltd.

This privacy information must include clear disclosure that AI tools (including OpenAI) are used to help review documents and fact-find responses submitted by data subjects, and that AI-generated suggestions are reviewed by a member of the Controller's team before being used or acted on — this applies whether or not the underlying data is special category data. This is a transparency requirement only; no consent is needed for it. Suggested wording that satisfies this element is provided at Annex 1, which the Controller may adopt or adapt for its own privacy policy; using it does not, by itself, discharge the Controller's wider obligations under this Section 07, and as between the parties, responsibility for the content, accuracy and currency of the Controller's own privacy policy rests with the Controller alone. Go Automation AI Ltd is not the Controller's legal adviser and does not review the Controller's privacy policy for compliance.

Where special category data is processed (Section 5), the Controller additionally confirms it has identified a valid Article 9 UK GDPR condition — in practice, for protection/insurance fact-find journeys, this means obtaining the data subject's explicit consent before that data is collected or processed — and has captured evidence of that consent, before submitting such data to the Platform. Go Automation implements a technical consent-gate step within the fact-find flow that requires the data subject to make a choice before health or lifestyle questions are presented, and records that choice as evidence. This is a recording mechanism only: Go Automation does not itself seek, obtain, or rely on that consent, and — because it is not the Controller — cannot be a party to it. The validity of the consent (that it is freely given, specific, informed and unambiguous) is assessed and owned entirely by the Controller; the gate does not determine or substitute for that assessment. This consent-gate applies only to the fact-find's health/lifestyle section — it has no bearing on, and does not extend to, the general AI-assisted review of documents described above, which requires disclosure but not consent.

Where the Controller instructs Go Automation to send electronic marketing communications (including WhatsApp messages) to any data subject, the Controller confirms that: the relevant contact details were collected directly by the Controller (not purchased or obtained from a third party); a valid lawful basis exists under the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR) — whether specific consent or the "soft opt-in" conditions — for that specific campaign; and recipients have been given the ability to opt out both at the point of collection and in every subsequent communication. Go Automation may require a completed Marketing/PECR Campaign Declaration before running any such campaign and may decline to run a campaign where this has not been provided or where the stated basis is evidently insufficient. Where a marketing recipient is a corporate subscriber (broadly, a limited company or LLP, as opposed to a sole trader or certain partnerships), the Controller's PECR analysis for that recipient may differ, since corporate subscribers do not benefit from the same PECR consent/soft-opt-in protections as individual subscribers — the Controller remains responsible for correctly classifying its recipients.

WhatsApp Business Account. The Platform sends and receives WhatsApp messages via a WhatsApp Business Account that the Controller connects directly to its own Meta Business Manager account. The Controller contracts with Meta directly for that account, and is solely responsible for: maintaining that account in good standing; accepting and complying with Meta's own applicable business and data processing terms for that account; and ensuring its use of WhatsApp complies with Meta's WhatsApp Business Policy. On this basis — the Controller, not Go Automation, determines and controls that relationship with Meta — Go Automation does not contract with Meta Platforms in respect of the Controller's WhatsApp Business Account, and treats Meta as the Controller's own provider rather than a sub-processor of Go Automation for this reason; see Section 10.

08Processor Obligations

  • Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller
  • Ensure all authorised persons are subject to confidentiality obligations
  • Ensure that every employee or contractor of Go Automation who may access Controller personal data is bound by written confidentiality and data protection obligations no less protective than those in this Agreement, and is given access only to the extent required for their role
  • Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures
  • Assist the Controller where reasonably necessary to comply with UK GDPR
  • Notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting Controller data
  • Maintain appropriate records of processing where required
  • Not use any AI development or testing tool that is not a listed sub-processor under Section 10 to process Controller personal data, including special category data
  • Automatically suppress further electronic marketing communications to any data subject who opts out (for example, by replying "STOP")

09Security Measures

  • Password-protected systems and role-based access controls
  • Multi-factor authentication on Platform user accounts held by Go Automation personnel and contractors
  • Least-privilege access: each person's permissions in a Controller's account are limited to what their role requires, reviewed regularly, and removed promptly when no longer needed
  • Go Automation personnel and contractors may not export, download or copy Controller personal data to personal devices or storage, other than as required to deliver the Services on the Controller's instructions
  • Each person accesses the Platform through their own named user account (no shared logins), so changes made in a Controller's account are recorded against that individual in the Platform's audit logs
  • Secure cloud-hosted infrastructure with encrypted communications
  • Regular backups, software updates and security monitoring
  • Authentication controls and logging of platform activity

10Approved Sub-Processors

  • GoHighLevel — CRM and automation platform, the technical platform used to send/receive messages via the Controller's own connected WhatsApp Business Account, and delivery of platform-generated SMS and email messages
  • OpenAI — AI language model processing of documents, fact-find content (including special category data described in Section 5), and conversational messaging
  • Google — communications and productivity services (including Gmail and Google Meet), and temporary storage (via Google Drive) of a Controller-supplied customer spreadsheet pending CRM import
  • Anthropic — where a Controller sends Go Automation a spreadsheet of its customers' contact details for CRM import, Go Automation's business Claude Team account may be used to check that data for accuracy (for example, values shifted between columns) before import. Covered by Anthropic's Commercial Terms of Service and accompanying Data Processing Addendum
  • Individual contractors engaged by Go Automation — independent contractors who provide configuration, testing and support of the Controller's CRM account and automations, and who may therefore access Controller personal data in the course of that work. Each is engaged under a written agreement containing data protection, confidentiality and security obligations no less protective than this Agreement, and is subject to the measures in Section 09. Contractor roles and locations (including any located outside the United Kingdom) are listed in the sub-processor register referred to below.

Go Automation AI Ltd shall ensure that any appointed sub-processor is subject to appropriate contractual obligations where required by law, and shall give the Controller at least 30 days' notice before appointing a new sub-processor that will process Controller personal data, during which the Controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds. A current list of sub-processors is maintained at goautomation.ai/subprocessors.

Go Automation personnel working on separate products. Go Automation's software developers who work solely on its document management product do not have access to a Controller's CRM account or CRM data unless the Controller also uses that product, in which case their access is limited to the data processed through that product and is subject to the measures in Sections 08 and 09.

Meta Platforms is treated as the Controller's own provider, not a sub-processor of Go Automation. Each Controller contracts with Meta directly and connects its own WhatsApp Business Account, under its own Meta Business Manager account, directly to its GoHighLevel sub-account — Go Automation never instructs Meta to process data on its own behalf, and has no contract with Meta in respect of that account. This role-allocation position rests on the Controller determining and controlling that specific relationship, not merely on whose account it is, and is kept under review against the actual technical data flow; it is further supported by Meta's absence from GoHighLevel's own published sub-processor list. The Controller's direct relationship with Meta for its own connected account remains the Controller's own responsibility (see Section 07). Message content is, however, still processed by Go Automation's own sub-processors (GoHighLevel, OpenAI) once it reaches the Platform.

Microsoft is not a sub-processor of Go Automation. Go Automation holds a personal Microsoft 365 subscription (Word/Excel) for internal use only; no Controller or Controller-customer personal data is ever stored in, sent through, or processed by Microsoft's cloud services.

Go Automation AI Ltd's internal software development and testing tools (which may include Anthropic's Claude, used without any real Controller personal data) are not sub-processors under this Agreement for that engineering use — this is distinct from the specific, documented business use of Anthropic's Claude Team account described above.

11International Transfers

Where Go Automation AI Ltd's own sub-processors (Section 10) transfer personal data outside the United Kingdom, Go Automation AI Ltd shall ensure appropriate safeguards are implemented in accordance with UK GDPR. A register of these sub-processors, their processing locations, and the applicable transfer safeguard is maintained and available to the Controller on request; where more than one transfer mechanism could apply to a given sub-processor, the register identifies the mechanism actually relied upon for each transfer rather than treating multiple available mechanisms as cumulative. This does not extend to the Controller's own direct relationship with Meta Platforms for its connected WhatsApp Business Account (see Section 07), which is the Controller's own responsibility to assess.

This includes access to Controller personal data by Go Automation's contractors located outside the United Kingdom, including remote access to the Platform. For each such contractor, Go Automation shall put in place the ICO's International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, supported by a transfer risk assessment, before that contractor is given access to Controller personal data, and shall record the contractor's location and the safeguard relied upon in the register referred to above.

12Confidentiality

All personal data processed on behalf of the Controller shall be treated as confidential. Go Automation AI Ltd shall not disclose personal data except where required to deliver the contracted services, where required by law, or where instructed by the Controller.

13Artificial Intelligence

Go Automation AI Ltd provides AI-powered automation using OpenAI as its AI language model provider. AI services operate only within workflows configured by the Controller and solely for the purpose of delivering the contracted services. Personal data processed through AI services, including special category data described in Section 5, is used for no other purpose. Go Automation AI Ltd does not intentionally use Controller personal data to train publicly available AI models without the Controller's explicit agreement.

14Data Subject Rights

Go Automation AI Ltd shall provide reasonable assistance to enable the Controller to respond to Subject Access Requests, Rectification Requests, Erasure Requests, Restriction Requests, Objection Requests, and Data Portability Requests. This assistance may be provided via a manual process rather than an automated tool; Go Automation maintains an internal procedure (the DSAR Handling Procedure) describing how such requests are actually handled operationally.

15Personal Data Breaches

If Go Automation AI Ltd becomes aware of a personal data breach affecting Controller data, it shall notify the Controller without undue delay and provide reasonable information to assist the Controller in meeting its legal obligations.

16Retention and Deletion

Go Automation AI Ltd retains personal data processed on behalf of the Controller in accordance with the Controller's documented instructions and any retention periods configured by the Controller within the Platform, reflecting that different Controllers (for example, mortgage brokers, accountants, and other regulated professional services firms) may be subject to different statutory, regulatory, or professional record-keeping requirements. Go Automation AI Ltd does not independently determine how long a Controller is required to retain its customers' records. Separate, shorter retention periods apply to temporary processing copies, AI-processing logs, and other operational data, as described in the Privacy Policy. Upon termination of the Service Agreement, Go Automation AI Ltd shall, at the Controller's instruction and subject to applicable legal requirements, securely delete personal data or make it available to the Controller for export before deletion, whether by automated or manual means; data remaining in routine backup copies may be retained and deleted according to Go Automation's documented backup cycle, rather than requiring instantaneous erasure, provided it is protected and put beyond ordinary operational use in the interim.

17Audits

Upon reasonable written request, Go Automation AI Ltd shall make available information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Agreement.

18Liability

Nothing within this Agreement limits or excludes liability where such limitation is prohibited by law.

19Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of England and Wales.

A1Annex 1 · Suggested Privacy Notice Wording — AI-Assisted Processing

This wording is provided by Go Automation AI Ltd as a starting point for the Controller's own privacy policy, reflecting the AI-assisted processing available through the Platform as at the Effective Date of this Agreement. It does not, by itself, satisfy every element of the Controller's privacy-information obligation under Section 07 — the Controller remains responsible for reviewing, adapting (including to reflect the specific Services it has actually subscribed to), and keeping current its own privacy policy, and for identifying any additional disclosure its own processing requires. Go Automation AI Ltd accepts no liability for the content of the Controller's privacy policy, whether or not this wording is used.

The first paragraph below is a transparency disclosure — no consent is required for it, only that Customers are told. The second paragraph (health/lifestyle information) describes consent that the Controller must obtain before any such information is collected; Go Automation's in-app fact-find consent gate records that choice but does not obtain it on the Controller's behalf (see Section 07).

How we use AI to process your information

We use artificial intelligence (AI) tools, including OpenAI, to help us review documents you provide (such as payslips, mortgage offers, and other supporting evidence) and to suggest information that may be relevant to your application. AI is never used to make decisions on your behalf — every suggestion is reviewed by a member of our team before any information is used or acted on.

Where your application relates to protection or insurance, we may also use AI to help review documents containing health, medical or lifestyle information. We will only do this with your specific, separate consent, which you will be asked to give before any such information is collected.
