How it works

Intelligent. Timed. Automatic.

Not a reminder. A fully managed client journey that gets to your clients first, educates them on the importance of a market review and books the appointment, automatically.

1

We Load Your Back Book and New Completions

Upload your client list with mortgage expiry dates and lender details. We handle the import and setup. One-off onboarding, done by us. Monthly data updates or seamless integration with your CRM.

2

We Keep in Touch Throughout the Fixed Rate Term

Throughout the fixed rate period, the AI communicates with clients via WhatsApp, gradually educating them on the importance of a full market review rather than an automatic lender switch. Newsletters, birthday and holiday messages keep your brand front of mind and support Consumer Duty compliance throughout the term. At six months, the appointment booking AI kicks in and takes over.

3

AI Contacts Clients 6 Months Out via WhatsApp

At six months before expiry, the appointment booking AI begins. Your client receives a personalised WhatsApp message explaining why they shouldn't automatically renew with their lender, priming them for a full market review before the lender's email arrives. The AI triages the client. If additional needs are identified, such as raising further funds or moving home, the appointment is booked immediately. If no additional needs are identified, the appointment is booked for the exact lender rate release date so your adviser can compare the rate.

4

Appointment Booked Directly Into Adviser Calendars

We integrate with Outlook, Gmail and Calendly so the AI can see live adviser availability and push appointments directly into their calendar, complete with video conferencing links for Zoom, Teams or Google Meet. The adviser and client both receive confirmation and reminders automatically.

5

Adviser Turns Up Informed

Before the meeting, the AI gathers updates on the client's situation, including any changes in income, plans to move, or additional borrowing needs. Your adviser walks in prepared and ready to advise.

6

Pipeline Builds Automatically

Each month, new completions are added via your CRM integration or a simple monthly data share. Your remortgage pipeline builds itself with no ongoing manual effort.

GDPR and compliance

Built for regulated firms.

Go Automation is designed from the ground up for FCA-regulated mortgage brokers. Every aspect of the platform has been built with compliance in mind.

You Remain Data Controller

Your client data is processed solely to deliver your contracted services. Go Automation acts as Data Processor. Data is never used for any other purpose without your explicit instruction.

Full Audit Trail

Every WhatsApp message is logged, timestamped, and stored centrally. You have a fully searchable conversation history ready for any FCA or FOS request at any time.

Data Processing Agreement

A full Data Processing Agreement is provided to every client, setting out clearly how data is handled, stored, and protected in compliance with UK GDPR.

Advisers Off Personal Phones

Advisers send and receive WhatsApp messages through our app or desktop software, not their personal phones. This significantly reduces your firm's compliance exposure and keeps all conversations in one auditable place.

Consumer Duty Evidenced

Regular client contact throughout the mortgage term is logged and evidenced, supporting your Consumer Duty obligations and demonstrating that clients were kept appropriately informed.

Secure Cloud Infrastructure

All data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. On termination of your contract, your data is returned or securely deleted at your instruction, and no personal data is retained beyond what is legally required. We keep only short document-type descriptions that contain no personal data — never names, account numbers, dates or amounts.
