01aWhose Data This Policy Covers

This policy has two audiences, and different parts of it apply to each:

  • If you are a broker, a prospective client, or a business contact of ours — for example you've filled in our contact form, you're a named user at a client firm, or we correspond with you directly — this whole policy applies to you, and we are the Data Controller of your personal data.
  • If you are a customer of one of our broker clients — for example you're a mortgage applicant whose broker uses our platform to message you or process your documents — your broker is the Data Controller of your personal data, and we act only as their Data Processor, under instructions set out in our Data Processing Agreement with them. This policy describes, for transparency, what our platform does with data it processes on a broker's behalf (see §6 and §9), but it does not replace your broker's own privacy notice — contact your broker directly to exercise your data rights, and see §10 for what happens if you contact us instead.

06Who We Share Your Data With

  • GoHighLevel — CRM and automation platform

We do not sell personal data to third parties. We require all third-party processors, including OpenAI, to handle data under contractual terms that prohibit using it to train their general-purpose models and that require appropriate security safeguards. Full detail is set out in our Data Processing Agreement.

Our engineers may separately use AI-assisted development and testing tools, including Anthropic's Claude, to help build and troubleshoot our platform. These tools are not used to process client or customer data as part of delivering the Services, and our internal policy prohibits inputting real personal data into them.

07International Transfers

Some of our third-party providers operate outside the United Kingdom. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with UK GDPR, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent mechanisms.

08How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Client account data: retained for the duration of the contract and up to 6 years thereafter
  • Enquiry and contact data: up to 2 years from last contact
  • Marketing data: until you withdraw consent or opt out
  • Legal and financial records: as required by law (typically 6–7 years)
  • Content processed by AI providers: retained by those providers only as long as their own operational/API log retention terms specify — see our Data Processing Agreement for current sub-processor terms.
  • PII-free document-type descriptions: to improve document classification accuracy over time, after a document has been confirmed as a particular type, we generate a short description of that document's type and appearance only (for example, "a bank statement showing an account header and transaction table") and store it, along with a numerical representation of that description, indefinitely — including after your broker's contract with us ends. This description is designed to exclude personal data entirely — it never includes names, account numbers, dates, or amounts — so it is not personal data about you and is not subject to the deletion timelines that apply elsewhere in this section.

09Artificial Intelligence

Our platform uses AI services provided by OpenAI. Depending on the feature, this may involve sending content you or your broker's customers submit — including documents such as financial statements or identification records, and information provided during a protection fact-find, including health and lifestyle information — to OpenAI, so that our platform can classify, summarise, extract information from, or generate responses relating to that content.

We select AI providers on terms that prohibit using data submitted through their business/API products to train their publicly available models, and we do not instruct any AI provider to use your data for any purpose beyond delivering the Services. AI processing operates only within workflows we have configured for the contracted Services.

Where health or lifestyle information is processed by AI as part of a protection fact-find, this only happens because your broker has obtained the appropriate consent from you for that specific purpose — see §01a for who is responsible for that.

AI-generated outputs (summaries, classifications, extracted data, drafted messages) may occasionally be inaccurate and are subject to review as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

To improve accuracy for your firm specifically, once a document has been confirmed as a particular type, we use OpenAI to generate a short, PII-free description of that document's type and appearance, and store this (with a numerical representation of it) to help classify similar future documents. This description never contains personal data values — see §8 for how long it's retained.

10Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to, and port your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@goautomation.ai. We will respond within one calendar month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the ICO at ico.org.uk.

If you are a mortgage applicant or other customer of one of our broker clients (see §01a), please direct data rights requests to your broker in the first instance, as they are the Data Controller. If you contact us directly, we will forward your request to the relevant broker without undue delay and assist them in responding, in line with our Data Processing Agreement.

11Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including access controls, encrypted communications, secure cloud infrastructure and regular security monitoring. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12Changes to This Policy
