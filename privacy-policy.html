01aWhose Data This Policy Covers

This policy has two audiences, and different parts of it apply to each:

  • If you are a Client, a prospective Client, or a business contact of ours — for example you've filled in our contact form, you're a named user at a Client firm (such as a mortgage broker, accountancy practice, or other professional services firm), or we correspond with you directly — this whole policy applies to you, and we are the Data Controller of your personal data.
  • If you are a customer of one of our Clients — for example you're a mortgage applicant, an accountancy client, or another individual whose adviser or professional firm uses our platform to message you or process your documents — your Client is the Data Controller of your personal data, and we act only as their Data Processor, under instructions set out in our Data Processing Agreement with them. This policy describes, for transparency, what our platform does with data it processes on a Client's behalf (see §6 and §9), but it does not replace your Client's own privacy notice — contact them directly to exercise your data rights, and see §10 for what happens if you contact us instead.

06Who We Share Your Data With

  • GoHighLevel — our CRM and automation platform, the technical platform used to send/receive WhatsApp messages via your Client's own connected WhatsApp Business Account, and delivery of platform-generated SMS and email messages.
  • OpenAI — processes documents and content submitted to our platform by you or your Client's customers, in order to classify, summarise, extract information from, and verify facts within that content, and to support conversational AI messaging, as part of delivering the Services. This may include health or lifestyle information where you have taken part in a protection fact-find journey, and may incidentally include other special category information that source documents or conversations happen to contain (see §09).
  • Google — communications and productivity services (including Gmail for our own business correspondence), Google Meet for video conferencing, and Google Drive, which we sometimes use to temporarily store a spreadsheet a Client has sent us of their customers' contact details, before importing it into GoHighLevel on the Client's instruction.
  • Anthropic (Claude Team) — our business Claude account is sometimes used to help check a Client-supplied customer spreadsheet for data-integrity issues (for example, values that have shifted between columns) before it's imported into GoHighLevel. This is covered by Anthropic's Commercial Terms of Service, which include a GDPR-compliant Data Processing Addendum incorporating Standard Contractual Clauses and a UK Addendum, and which contractually prohibit using your data to train Anthropic's models.
  • Professional advisers (legal, accountancy, insurance)
  • Regulatory bodies or law enforcement where required by law

WhatsApp messages are sent and received via your Client's own WhatsApp Business Account, connected to their own Meta Business Manager account — your Client contracts with Meta directly for this, and Go Automation does not. On this basis, we treat Meta as your Client's own provider rather than one of our sub-processors, a position we keep under review against how the integration actually works technically. Once a message reaches our platform, its content is processed by our own sub-processors listed above (GoHighLevel, OpenAI) in the same way as any other content.

We do not sell personal data to third parties. We require all third-party processors, including OpenAI, GoHighLevel, Google, and Anthropic, to handle data under contractual terms that prohibit using it to train their general-purpose models and that require appropriate security safeguards. Full detail is set out in our Data Processing Agreement, including our current sub-processor list at goautomation.ai/subprocessors.

Our engineers may separately use AI-assisted development and testing tools, including Anthropic's Claude, to help build and troubleshoot our platform — this internal engineering use never involves real Client or Client-customer data, and our internal policy prohibits inputting it there. This is distinct from the specific, documented business use of Claude described above.

We also hold a personal Microsoft 365 subscription (Word/Excel), used internally for general office tasks such as drafting documents. This is never used to store, send, or process Client or Client-customer personal data — anything containing such data is saved to Google Drive or kept locally instead — so Microsoft does not process personal data on our behalf and is not one of our sub-processors.

07International Transfers

Some of our own sub-processors (§06) operate outside the United Kingdom. Where personal data is transferred internationally through them, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with UK GDPR, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, or an applicable adequacy mechanism. A register of our sub-processors, their processing locations, and the relevant safeguard is available on request. This does not cover your Client's own direct relationship with Meta Platforms for their connected WhatsApp Business Account, which is a separate matter for your Client to address in their own privacy notice.

08How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Client account data: retained for the duration of the contract and up to 6 years thereafter
  • Enquiry and contact data: up to 2 years from last contact
  • Marketing data: until you withdraw consent or opt out
  • Legal and financial records: as required by law (typically 6–7 years)
  • Documents and records stored on the platform on behalf of a Client (for example, a mortgage applicant's bank statements, or an accountancy client's records): retained in accordance with the retention period configured or instructed by that Client, reflecting that different Clients have different statutory, regulatory, or professional record-keeping obligations. We do not independently decide how long a Client must keep its customers' records — see our Data Processing Agreement.
  • A Client-supplied spreadsheet of their customers' contact details, sent to us to import into GoHighLevel: retained only for a short validation window — the email and its attachment are kept for up to 30 days after the import has been checked and confirmed correct, after which the attachment (or the whole email, where practical) is deleted. We don't keep this indefinitely, since it's a complete copy of your customers' contact details, not ordinary correspondence.
  • Content processed by AI providers: retained by those providers only as long as their own operational/API log retention terms specify — see our Data Processing Agreement for current sub-processor terms.
  • PII-free document-type descriptions: to improve document classification accuracy over time, after a document has been confirmed as a particular type, we generate a short description of that document's type and appearance only (for example, "a bank statement showing an account header and transaction table") and store it, along with a numerical representation of that description, indefinitely — including after your Client's contract with us ends. This description is designed to exclude personal data entirely — it never includes names, account numbers, dates, or amounts — so it is not personal data about you and is not subject to the deletion timelines that apply elsewhere in this section.

09Artificial Intelligence

Our platform uses AI services provided by OpenAI. Depending on the feature, this may involve sending content you or your Client's customers submit — including documents such as financial statements or identification records, and information provided during a protection fact-find, including health and lifestyle information — to OpenAI, so that our platform can classify, summarise, extract information from, or generate responses relating to that content.

Ordinary documents and conversations are not intentionally used to collect special category data outside an approved fact-find journey, but they may incidentally contain it — for example, a bank statement showing a payment to a religious, political, or medical organisation, or a customer voluntarily mentioning a health condition during an otherwise unrelated conversation. The same safeguards described in this section apply to any such information.

We select AI providers on terms that prohibit using data submitted through their business/API products to train their publicly available models, and we do not instruct any AI provider to use your data for any purpose beyond delivering the Services. AI processing operates only within workflows we have configured for the contracted Services.

Where health or lifestyle information is processed by AI as part of a protection fact-find, this only happens because your Client has obtained the appropriate consent from you for that specific purpose — see §01a for who is responsible for that.

AI-generated outputs (summaries, classifications, extracted data, drafted messages) may occasionally be inaccurate and are subject to review as set out in our Terms & Conditions.

To improve accuracy for your Client specifically, once a document has been confirmed as a particular type, we use OpenAI to generate a short, PII-free description of that document's type and appearance, and store this (with a numerical representation of it) to help classify similar future documents. This description never contains personal data values — see §8 for how long it's retained.

If you are a customer of one of our Clients (see §01a), please direct data rights requests to that Client in the first instance, as they are the Data Controller. If you contact us directly, we will forward your request to the relevant Client without undue delay and assist them in responding, in line with our Data Processing Agreement.
